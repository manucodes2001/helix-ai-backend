## 2. Standardized Interview Questionnaire
1. **Current Literature Search Workflow:** Which databases and search strategies do you use first when starting a new biomedical topic, and how do you track retrieved papers?
2. **Dataset Discovery:** How do you currently find gene expression datasets (NCBI GEO, ArrayExpress) associated with papers you read?
3. **Cross-Referencing Effort:** When a paper mentions a dataset or cohort, what steps do you take to verify the exact `GSE` accession, platform (`GPL`), and sample count?
4. **Structured Data Extraction:** How do you extract experimental metadata (organism, sample size, methodology, biomarkers, pathways) into comparison tables?
5. **Citation & Claim Verification:** How often do you encounter misattributed citations or unsupported claims in literature reviews or AI tools, and how do you verify them?
6. **Time Allocation Bottlenecks:** Across searching, dataset linking, table extraction, and verification, which step consumes the most hours per week?
7. **Workflow Validation:** Which Helix AI capability (automated `PMID <-> GSE` linking, structured provenance tables, or claim-level verification flags) would save the most time in your daily research?
